Meet the Barber

Max, known as @pirate.barber on Instagram, is the man behind the chair. Based in Logan, Utah, and cutting at Boneyard Barbering, Max brings skill, style, and a sharp eye to every client who sits down.

Whether you need a clean fade, a classic taper, or a hot towel shave, Max treats every cut like a craft. His reputation in the Northern Utah barber scene speaks for itself.
